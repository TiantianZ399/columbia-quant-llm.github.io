Community Background

The community currently includes members with experience at Jump Trading, JQ Investments, DeepMind, Meta, AlphaGrep, Google, Citadel Securities, Kimi / Moonshot AI, Qwen, and Tencent Hunyuan, Deepseek. Disclaimer: this is a learning seminar, not networking event, no commercial usage, no compilance issue.

The community also includes contributors from MathHeart and Mochi(main instructor) community.

Members are encouraged to introduce themselves and share work related to LLMs, learning theory, post-training, verification, reasoning, AI systems, OPD, post-training harnesses, or quantitative research.

Open Contributors

Contributors may help with reading notes, paper selection, website maintenance, replication code, seminar organization, and research discussions.
